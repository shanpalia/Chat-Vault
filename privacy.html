Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Chat Vault is an Android application by PaliaAPK HUB. This page explains how the app handles chat export files selected by you.

Files you choose

Chat Vault processes TXT and ZIP chat exports that you deliberately select through Android's system file picker. Imported files and extracted media are stored in app-private storage on your device.

WhatsApp access

Chat Vault does not silently access WhatsApp's private chat database, messages, contacts, or account. You must export a chat from WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business and select that export in Chat Vault.

Sharing and collection

The app's core chat import and viewing features are designed to work on your device. Do not import files you do not want stored on the device. If you choose to share a file using Android's share features, that action is controlled by you and the receiving app.

Advertising

The app may display Google AdMob advertisements. Google may process device and advertising information as described in Google's own privacy disclosures and controls. See Google Privacy Policy.

Data retention and deletion

Imported data remains in the app's private storage until you remove it through the app or clear/uninstall the app. Keep your own copy of any export you want to retain.

Children

Chat Vault is a general utility app and is not specifically directed to children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the app changes. For questions, contact the developer through the Chat Vault GitHub repository.
